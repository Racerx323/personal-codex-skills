Local changes combine overlapping pattern guidance, add drafting, preserve
technical literals and operational boundaries, retain meaningful uncertainty,
permit useful passive voice and nonhuman subjects, and make change commentary
conditional. Detection reports observable patterns without claiming AI authorship.
No upstream scripts, plugins, or executable helpers are included in this skill.

Unslop informs stance preservation, contextual treatment of redundant hedges and
boilerplate, sample-based voice guidance, editing scope, and the evaluation
protocol. Sources are `skills/unslop/SKILL.md`, `skills/unslop-review/SKILL.md`,
`.cursor/skills/unslop/anti-aiisms.md`, `.cursor/skills/unslop/prompt-templates.md`,
and `evals/perceived_humanness.py` at the pinned revision above. The adaptation
uses contextual decisions rather than word bans, rhythm quotas, detector targets,
or persistent activation. Its examples are local synthetic examples.
