# Final check

Compare the result with the source and the user's request.

1. Are factual claims, quantities, negation, uncertainty, and causal strength unchanged?
2. Are commands, identifiers, paths, code, URLs, and direct quotations preserved?
3. Are prerequisites, authorization boundaries, and incomplete work still explicit?
4. Does the edit preserve the writer's vocabulary, rhythm, and useful structure?
5. Did removing filler avoid inventing evidence, sources, examples, or an actor?
6. Does the output match draft, edit, or detection mode and the requested format?
7. Have any real information gaps been flagged without unnecessary questions?

Correct a failure before delivering. If the source cannot support a needed
statement, ask or report the gap. Do not assign a numerical writing-quality score
or infer AI authorship from stylistic patterns.
